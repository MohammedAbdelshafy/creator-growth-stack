Free lead-file health checklist

Try this on a small synthetic or already-authorized sample first. You can use these questions even if you never hire us.

  1. Ownership: Can you explain where each record came from and whether the data may be used for your intended purpose?
  2. Column dictionary: Do you know what each field means and which ones are essential?
  3. Duplicate rules: Is a duplicate defined by email, a combination of fields, or an approved business-specific rule?
  4. Missing values: Are empty contact details, source labels and important timestamps flagged rather than guessed?
  5. Suppression: Can you exclude opted-out, invalid, disputed or ineligible records before any outreach?
  6. Provenance: Can every cleaned row be traced to the original, with an exceptions log?
  7. Next actions: Are proposed follow-ups subject to a human compliance and business review?
What this is not: This checklist does not verify that a phone number is active, that an email is deliverable, or that a prospect has consented to marketing. A visible tracking tag on a public website does not reveal private visitor identities.
