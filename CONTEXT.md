# Creator Growth Stack CONTEXT

One job: present a scoped, truthful offer for existing creator-facing tools without implying that separate prototypes already form one live pipeline.

## Inputs
- Landing page: [index.html](index.html)
- Scope and price caveats: [README](README.md)
- Supporting content tool: [ClipOps Studio](https://github.com/MohammedAbdelshafy/clipops-studio)
- Sales workflows: [SalesOS](https://github.com/MohammedAbdelshafy/ai-sales-os)
- Data cleanup: [Veridata](https://github.com/MohammedAbdelshafy/dataclean-ai)

## Process
1. Identify the one business problem the creator wants solved first.
2. Verify which supporting tool can actually deliver that task today.
3. Offer a narrow paid scope only when the deliverable and payment path are confirmed.
4. Keep bundle pricing as a hypothesis until delivery and customer demand are proven.
5. Track delivery time, revisions, direct cost and customer acceptance.

## Output
A scoped offer and a clear next step, not an implied end-to-end pipeline that has not been verified.

## Check
The page reflects current code and delivery readiness. No planned feature, fabricated customer outcome or unverified revenue claim is presented as live.
