How the stack works

1

Content in. You bring the long-form: a video, a podcast, a webinar. ClipOps Studio runs it through a 7-stage agent pipeline — analysis, brand, script, motion, build, choreography, review — and renders platform-ready promo clips.

2

Shorts out. Every stage leaves a readable, editable artifact you can overrule. The video itself is a data structure rendered by template — consistent branding, every time.

3

Leads captured. Viewers become rows. AI Sales OS ingests your opportunity list, qualifies every row against your declared ICP with per-row evidence, and builds a deadline-driven next-action queue.

4

Follow-up, no leaks. Stale deals, missing fields, and suppressed contacts surface in a manager exception report — so no lead quietly dies in the spreadsheet.

5

Revenue-ready data. DataClean AI normalizes, dedupes, and verifies the list — up to 10,000 records in 48 hours — and hands back a quality report your team can audit.
